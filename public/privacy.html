Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WAVE ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the WAVE application and related services. By using WAVE, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store your plain-text password)
  • Profile Information: Profile avatar image you choose to upload
  • Community Content: Communities you create, their names, descriptions, tags, and settings
  • Messages: Direct messages and community channel messages you send, including any files or images attached
  • Payment Information: When you subscribe to a paid community or connect a Stripe account as a creator, payment details are processed directly by Stripe, Inc. We do not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials on our servers
  • Reports: Information you submit when reporting other users

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, communities joined, and interactions within the app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and app version
  • Connection Data: IP address, timestamps of logins and activity, and socket connection data for real-time features
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you enable push notifications, we store your device's push subscription object to deliver notifications
  • Last Seen: The timestamp of your most recent activity, used to show online/offline status to friends

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Stripe: When you connect a Stripe account or make a payment, Stripe provides us with identifiers (customer ID, subscription ID, account ID) to manage your subscription or creator account. We do not receive your full financial data from Stripe.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your WAVE account
  • Enable real-time voice rooms, messaging, and community features
  • Process subscription payments and payouts to creators via Stripe
  • Send you notifications about friend requests, messages, and community activity
  • Display your online status and last seen time to your friends
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
  • Respond to support requests and reports of abuse
  • Improve the performance, security, and features of WAVE
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Other Users

  • Your username and avatar are visible to other users in communities you join and rooms you participate in
  • Messages you send are visible to the recipients (direct messages) or channel members (community messages)
  • Your online/last seen status is visible to your friends

3.2 With Service Providers

  • Stripe, Inc.: For payment processing, creator payouts, and subscription management. Stripe's privacy policy applies to data they process: stripe.com/privacy
  • MongoDB Atlas: Our database provider stores your data on secure cloud infrastructure
  • Render: Our hosting provider runs the WAVE application servers

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of any person, prevent fraud, or protect our legal rights.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Messages: Retained until deleted by the sender or until the community is deleted
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations
  • Deleted content: Messages marked as deleted are hidden from users but may remain in backups for up to 90 days before permanent deletion

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Correction

You may update your username, email, and avatar at any time from your profile settings within the app.

5.2 Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data. To request deletion, contact us at privacy@wave-chat.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will process your request within 30 days. Note that payment records required for legal compliance will be retained per Section 4.

5.3 Push Notifications

You may disable push notifications at any time through your device settings or within the WAVE app.

5.4 Data Portability

You may request a copy of your data by contacting us at privacy@wave-chat.app.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

WAVE is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@wave-chat.app and we will delete the information promptly. Users must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • JWT tokens for secure authentication with expiration
  • Stripe-hosted payment processing (we never handle raw card data)
  • MongoDB Atlas security features including encryption at rest

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Payment Processors

WAVE uses Stripe exclusively for payment processing. We do not permit integration of third-party payment platforms, marketplace tools, or external monetization services (such as Whop, Patreon, or similar platforms) within the WAVE application for community subscriptions. All creator monetization flows through WAVE's built-in subscription system powered by Stripe and, on iOS devices, Apple's In-App Purchase system.

9. Apple App Store (iOS Users)

If you use WAVE through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect certain data in accordance with Apple's own privacy policy. Community subscription purchases made within the iOS app are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/privacy.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@wave-chat.app.

11. International Users

WAVE is operated in the United States. If you access WAVE from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using WAVE, you consent to this transfer.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice within the app or sending an email to your registered address. Continued use of WAVE after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
